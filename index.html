ABOUT PROLOGUE

Every story begins somewhere.

Perhaps yours begins here.

PROLOGUE is the reading club of LBSITW, created for students who enjoy books, literature, writing and meaningful conversations.

Through discussions, events and creative activities, PROLOGUE encourages students to read, express themselves and discover new perspectives.
